Spring Boot 4.x moved to a Spring Framework 7 baseline and Java 17 minimum; the deck's code is Boot 3.x-era (jakarta.* imports, lambda SecurityFilterChain DSL) and transfers to 4.x almost unchanged. The capstone

One application, built in fifteen increments: a Student Scholarship & Progress Management Platform. It was chosen because it forces every hard problem into the open — multi-role access control, document upload and consent, guardian relationships, auditability, reporting, and a search surface that will absolutely be slow if you model it carelessly.

Built so far: Stage 1 · Anatomy of a Request · Stage 2 · Types and the Event Loop · Stage 3 · The Rendering Model · Stage 4 · Screens That Survive · Stage 5 · Five Kinds of State · Stage 6 · The Container and the Language · Stage 7 · The Contract at the Edge · Stage 8 · The Cost of Every Query · Stage 9 · Who Are You, and May You · Stage 10 · Which Test Would Have Caught It · Stage 11 · Where Did the 900 Milliseconds Go · Stage 12 · It Was Slow at 14:32 · Stage 13 · Works On My Machine · Stage 14 · Merged Is Not Deployed · Stage 15 · Knowing When Not To. All fifteen stages are built. The atlas is complete — read it in order, or jump to whichever decision is in front of you.
